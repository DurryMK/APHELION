Quick guide

Celestial bodies

Dust merges on gentle contact. Planets grow by capturing and absorbing asteroids.

Life begins at 10 M. Population grows toward the mass your world can support; overcrowding slows research and reduces population.

Upkeep never breaks your world. Combat integrity below 30% is fatal; fresh mass helps repair it.

Sol and Pulsar can be mined at technology 7. Void cannot be mined.

Technology levels

  1. Incubation · Life takes 30 seconds to emerge.
  2. Shield · Protects your world.
  3. Point defense · Slow, heavy shots against close threats.
  4. One carrier · Three ships for combat and transport.
  5. Three carriers · Four ships each, with greater reach.
  6. Space city · Stronger shields, more living space and faster ship production.
  7. Double city · No population cap; stronger ships and larger cargo holds.
  8. Stellar mining · Harvest mass from Sol and Pulsar.

Ships mine asteroids and unshielded planets. Shields must be broken first. Cargo only becomes yours after unloading.

Ships need ammunition and life support. Recall before accelerating; ships that cannot return are lost.
