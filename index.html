Celestial bodies

Dust merges on gentle contact. Planets grow by capturing and absorbing asteroids.

More mass supports more life. Sol, Pulsar and Void are natural hazards.

Technology levels

  1. Shield · Protects your world.
  2. Lasers · Defend against nearby enemies.
  3. Artificial moons · Stronger, longer-range firepower.
  4. Space city · More living space and spatial jumps.
  5. Harvesting · Absorb half the remaining mass of planets destroyed by your lasers.
